Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Even - Habit App ("Even") collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the control you have over it. Even is for people 13 and older.

1. Who we are

Even is developed by William Phelps ("we," "us," "our"). Contact: evenhabitsapp@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and create a random account ID for you. If you sign up with email, we also collect a password, which is stored securely and never in plain text, and the two-factor authentication you set up. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (which may be a private relay address) and, if you choose to share it, your name. Google may also share a link to your profile photo. If you use Sign in with Apple, we keep an Apple authorization token on our servers only so we can revoke it when you delete your account. Accounts are handled by our backend provider, Supabase.

Habit and activity data

The habits you create, when you complete them, and any notes or other content you add, such as vacation and streak freeze dates. Your streaks and battery level are calculated from this. We use it only to run the app and show you your own progress.

Preferences and setup answers

Your mascot's name, your display name, reminder times, waking hours, display and notification settings, the starter habits you pick during setup, your answer to what you'd like help with, and the time you confirmed you're 13 or older. The goals you pick during setup stay on your phone and aren't sent to us.

Subscription data

If you subscribe, Apple (or Google Play on Android) processes your payment. We never see or store your payment card details. We receive your subscription status and purchase history (such as active, trial, renewed or expired, and when) through RevenueCat, our subscription provider. We share your account ID (not your email) with RevenueCat to link your subscription to your account.

Reminders

Reminders you turn on are scheduled on your device. We do not use push notifications and we do not store a push token. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing history. We do not read your photos: if you save a progress card, Even only adds that image to your photos. We do not collect weight, calorie, or other body data. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Provide and maintain your account and habit data
  • Manage your subscription
  • Find and fix problems you report to us
  • Send the reminders you turn on
  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data to train AI models, and we do not sell it.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. Each processes data only to provide its service to us, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase (database and sign-in): https://supabase.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management): https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple (App Store payments and Sign in with Apple): https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google (Google Play payments on Android, and Sign in with Google if you choose it): https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Where your data is stored

Your account and habit data is stored by Supabase in Canada. RevenueCat, Apple and Google may process your subscription and sign-in information in the United States and other countries. If you use Even from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in those countries.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app under Profile > Account Details > Delete Account. This is self-service and you do not need to contact us. Deletion cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel that first in your Apple or Google Play settings.

Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is erased. Some records may remain outside our systems under other companies' own policies, such as subscription records RevenueCat keeps and transaction records Apple and Google keep for their own legal purposes.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can do most of this in the app:

  • View your data: your habits and progress are visible in the app.
  • Delete your data: use account deletion (Section 6).
  • Export or correct your data: email evenhabitsapp@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal basis for using your data is providing the service you asked for, along with our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working.

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

8. Children's privacy

Even is for people 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us at evenhabitsapp@gmail.com.

9. Data security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission and row-level access controls so each account can only reach its own data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will also tell you in the app.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: evenhabitsapp@gmail.com
