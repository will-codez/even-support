Support

Even - Habit App is a habit tracker with a battery you keep charged with your buddy. If something isn't working, the answers below cover most questions. If they don't, email us.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google, so you cancel there. Deleting the app or your Even account doesn't cancel a subscription.

  • iPhone or iPad: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Even, then Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Even, then Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

I paid but Even still asks me to subscribe

Open Even, go to Profile, then Subscription, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with, and to the same Even account.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds go through them.

  • Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the Even purchase.
  • Google Play: follow Google's steps at support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

In Even, go to Profile, then Account Details, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it: habits, check-offs, notes and settings. It can't be undone. It doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel that first (see above).

Can't sign in? Email us from your account's email address and we'll delete it for you.

I lost my authenticator app and can't sign in

Email us from the email address on your account. We'll confirm it's you before removing two-factor authentication, so your account stays safe.

Who can use Even?

Even is for ages 13 and up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we'll delete it.

Using Even

How does the battery work?

Checking off your habits charges the battery, and it drains slowly during your waking hours. Doing all of today's habits adds up to 60%. Your waking hours are in Profile, then Preferences.

My reminders aren't showing up

Check that notifications for Even are on in your phone's settings (on iPhone: Settings, then Notifications, then Even). In Even, each habit has its own reminder under Remind me. Reminders only come on days the habit is scheduled, and only during your waking hours.
